📘 AI for 4th Graders (Through Senior Citizens): A Fun and Friendly Guide to Artificial Intelligence

Curious about AI but don’t speak “tech?” Whether you’re 9 or 99, AI for 4th Graders (through Senior Citizens) makes artificial intelligence simple, fun, and easy to understand.

This illustrated workbook walks readers through:

  • What AI is and how it “thinks”
  • How machines learn, listen, and talk
  • Real-life AI tools like ChatGPT and DALL·E
  • Creative hands-on projects (like building a chatbot!)
  • How to use AI ethically and responsibly

No math or coding skills needed — just curiosity and a pencil!

Whether you’re a student, teacher, parent, or lifelong learner, this book helps you become a Junior AI Explorer with quizzes, activities, and a certificate at the end.

👉 Perfect for homeschoolers, tech clubs, classroom enrichment, or just-for-fun learning.
