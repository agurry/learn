How to Buy a Domain (something.com) through Squarespace and Create a Website Using Google Sites

By Andrew Gurry · May 23, 2025 · Business
From the A-A Events archive. Originally published May 23, 2025 and migrated to Learn Andrew Gurry. Business rules, filing requirements, products, and pricing may have changed since publication. Verify current requirements with the relevant government agency or provider.

In the digital age, having a website is essential, whether you’re a business owner, blogger, or hobbyist. If you’re looking to purchase a domain through Squarespace and build your website using Google Sites, you’ve come to the right place. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you through the process.

Step 1: Buying Your Domain through Squarespace

  1. Visit Squarespace: Start by going to Squarespace. Squarespace is known for its sleek design and user-friendly interface, making it a great option for purchasing a domain.
  2. Search for Your Domain: Enter the domain name you wish to purchase in the search bar. Squarespace will show you the availability of the domain and suggest alternatives if your preferred choice is taken.
  3. Select Your Domain: Once you find an available domain that suits your needs, click on it to proceed. Squarespace will provide information on the cost and terms of the domain.
  4. Add to Cart and Check Out: After selecting your domain, add it to your cart and proceed to checkout. Squarespace will guide you through the process of entering your payment information and completing your purchase.
  5. Configure Your Domain: Once your purchase is complete, you can access your domain settings. Here, you can set up custom email addresses, configure domain privacy settings, and link your domain to Google Sites or other web services.

Step 2: Creating Your Website with Google Sites

  1. Access Google Sites: Go to Google Sites. If you have a Google account, you can log in and start creating your website right away.
  2. Start a New Site: Click on the “+” button to create a new site. Google Sites offers a range of templates to choose from, or you can start with a blank canvas.
  3. Customize Your Site:
    • Title and Header: Start by adding a title to your site and choosing a header image. You can upload your own image or choose from Google’s library.
    • Pages: Add pages to your site by clicking on the “Pages” tab. Common pages include Home, About, Services, Contact, and Blog.
    • Content: Use the “Insert” tab to add content to your pages. You can include text, images, videos, calendars, maps, and more. Google Sites makes it easy to drag and drop elements into place.
  4. Design and Layout: Customize the look and feel of your site by choosing a theme and adjusting the layout. Google Sites offers several themes that can give your site a professional appearance.
  5. Preview and Publish: Before publishing your site, use the preview feature to see how it will look on different devices. Make any necessary adjustments, then click “Publish.” You will be prompted to choose a web address for your site. Enter the domain you purchased through Squarespace.
  6. Manage Your Site: After publishing, you can continue to update and manage your site through Google Sites. You can add new content, edit existing pages, and monitor your site’s performance.

Tips for a Successful Website

  • Keep It Simple: Avoid clutter and focus on clear, concise content. A clean design helps visitors navigate your site easily.
  • High-Quality Images: Use high-resolution images that are relevant to your content. Poor-quality images can detract from your site’s professionalism.
  • Mobile-Friendly: Ensure your site looks great on all devices, including smartphones and tablets. Google Sites automatically adjusts for different screen sizes.
  • SEO Best Practices: Optimize your site for search engines by using relevant keywords, writing descriptive meta tags, and creating high-quality content.

Conclusion

Creating a website with a domain purchased through Squarespace and using Google Sites for the build is a user-friendly and efficient way to establish your online presence. This guide will help you navigate the process with ease, ensuring you have a professional and functional website up and running in no time. Enjoy sharing your ideas, promoting your business, or connecting with your audience through your new site.

By following these steps, you’ll have a polished and functional website ready to share with the world. Happy website building!
